CE-809

Structural Dynamics Virtual Lab

Fifteen linked experiments, from the equation of motion and resonance to numerical integration, nonlinear response, response spectra, multi-storey modal analysis, and tuned mass dampers. Each one has live diagrams, computed results, and an applied challenge.

  • Equation of motion
  • Free vibration
  • Resonance
  • Half-power damping
  • Base isolation
  • Fourier loading
  • Pulse loading
  • Numerical methods
  • Nonlinear response
  • Response spectra
  • State space
  • Two-DOF modes
  • Multi-storey modal analysis
  • Tuned mass damper
  • Challenges
