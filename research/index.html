Research

Intelligent methods for civil infrastructure

My research brings structural engineering, computer vision, and artificial intelligence together to improve how infrastructure is documented, inspected, and assessed.

Research area

AI for Infrastructure Inspection

I develop computer-vision and deep-learning methods for recognizing structural elements and damage in images and point clouds. The objective is to make inspection information more quantitative, repeatable, and useful to engineers.

  • Computer vision
  • Damage segmentation
  • Point clouds
  • Vision-language methods
Research area

Digital Twins & UAVs

This work uses UAV imagery, photogrammetry, LiDAR, and automated semantic processing to create digital representations of bridges and buildings. These representations connect geometry and inspection evidence for infrastructure decision-making.

  • UAV data acquisition
  • Photogrammetry
  • 3D reconstruction
  • Digital twins
Research area

Structural Dynamics & Resilience

My broader structural-engineering work includes vibration and dynamic response, condition monitoring, and probabilistic seismic hazard assessment for resilient civil infrastructure.

  • Structural dynamics
  • Seismic hazard
  • Condition monitoring
  • Resilience

Featured work

Published and ongoing work spanning the path from field data to inspection-ready engineering information.

Published · 2026

BridgeELSpect

BridgeELSpect is an automated element-level bridge-inspection workflow that uses UAV imagery, photogrammetric reconstruction, damage segmentation, 3D damage mapping, and geometry-driven quantification to produce condition-state information.

  • UAV imagery
  • 3D damage mapping
  • Element-level inspection
  • Condition states

Ongoing research

BridgeEQA-3D

BridgeEQA-3D is an ongoing research direction exploring embodied question answering for bridge inspection in three-dimensional environments. Further details will be added as the work becomes public.

The project builds on the publicly available BridgeEQA research on virtual embodied agents for real bridge inspections.

Published research

Digital Twins of Bridges

This research program combines reality capture, bridge-element segmentation, and inspection data to develop useful digital twins of existing bridges. It includes field collection using UAVs and methods trained on synthetic bridge point clouds.
