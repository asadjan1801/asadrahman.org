How anonymity works

The form does not ask for identity or contact information, and its email contains only the topic, optional course area, and message you provide. Cloudflare still processes ordinary connection information to deliver the page and prevent abuse, and the email has a delivery time. Avoid wording that could identify you if that matters to you.
