Structural Dynamics

Study how structures respond to time-varying loads through vibration theory, analytical models, numerical methods, and interactive experimentation.

Course overview

CE-809 develops the fundamental concepts and methods used to describe the dynamic behavior of structural systems. The course connects equations of motion and response calculations with the physical behavior of vibrating structures.

Learning focus

  • Formulation of equations of motion
  • Free vibration and damping
  • Harmonic loading and resonance
  • Base excitation and vibration isolation
  • Periodic and non-periodic loading
  • Numerical response methods
  • Response spectra and state-space systems
  • Mode shapes and structural control

Interactive virtual lab

The course lab provides live system diagrams, plots, computed results, engineering interpretations, and applied challenges. Students can vary model parameters and immediately observe how structural response changes.
