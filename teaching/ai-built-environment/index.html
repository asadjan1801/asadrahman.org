Artificial Intelligence in the Built Environment

An introduction to artificial intelligence, machine learning, and data-driven problem solving for civil engineering and the built environment.

All Courses

Course overview

CE-312 introduces the foundations of artificial intelligence and shows how data-driven methods can support analysis, monitoring, inspection, and decision-making in the built environment.

Learning focus

  • AI and machine-learning foundations
  • Engineering data and preprocessing
  • Supervised and unsupervised learning
  • Model evaluation and interpretation
  • Computer vision for civil engineering
  • Responsible use of AI in engineering
